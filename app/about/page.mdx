# About

**Belonging through action.** We’re a community exploration & conservation group hosting approachable, alcohol-free gatherings—coffee tastings, urban walks, hands-on workshops—and simple ways to volunteer with neighbors.

## What we value

- **Welcoming by default.** First-timers should feel like regulars.
- **Doing over talking.** Learn by touching, tasting, trying.
- **Local first.** Support people and places around us.
- **Gentle sustainability.** Small habits add up.

## How we show up

- Short, on-time events with clear descriptions.
- Sliding-scale or free whenever possible.
- Friendly check-in, gentle intros, no pressure to mingle.

> “The best community is the one you actually go to.”

If you’d like to help organize or host, email **hello@example.org**.
